The Problem

The Industry Runs on
a Broken Assumption

Every major fund administrator validates counterparty cashflows the same flawed way. If your number matches their number — it must be correct. But what if both are wrong?

01

Interest Leakage

Errors in loan notices, swap payments, and prime brokerage statements go undetected for months. Basis-point losses compound silently and erode fund performance.

02

False Positive Breaks

Teams spend 80% of their time clearing reconciliation breaks that are timing differences, not real errors. Real errors hide in the noise and go unrecovered.

03

Linear Scaling

Every new strategy or AUM milestone requires proportionally more headcount. Growth is permanently capped by the humans you can hire and train.

04

Unstructured Blindspot

Credit agreements and ISDA confirmations are PDFs. Legacy systems can't read them — so they never verify the math. They just match two potentially wrong numbers.

The Tool

3-Way Validation.
Contract as Truth.

Unlike every reconciliation tool on the market, this goes back to the original legal agreement — recomputes what the cashflow should be — then challenges the counterparty if it's wrong.

1

AI Document Parser

Reads credit agreements & ISDA confirmations in raw PDF — extracts rate, spread, day-count, payment schedule automatically.

2

Contractual Recomputation Engine

Independently calculates expected cashflows from legal terms across syndicated loans, OTC derivatives, prime brokerage, and fixed income.

3

Adversarial Challenge Layer

Auto-generates formal discrepancy notices with contractual citations when computed amounts diverge from counterparty statements.

4

Agentic Exception Management

Routes only true breaks to human reviewers. Everything else auto-resolved with full audit trail — eliminating the false-positive hamster wheel.

Method and System for Adversarial Validation of Financial Counterparty Cashflow Statements Using Contractual Terms as the Authoritative Source of Truth
Inventor: Harsha Balaji
Filed: May 2026
Status: Provisional Application Filed
Office: USPTO

Computer-implemented methods for independently validating the mathematical accuracy of cashflow statements issued by financial counterparties — including agent banks, prime brokers, custodians, and clearing houses — by recomputing expected cashflows from executed contractual terms and adversarially challenging stated figures where discrepancies are identified. The concept: a 3-way reconciliation with the original contract as the authoritative source of truth.
